# 映汐放映站 0.2.0-alpha.53 / Yingxi 0.2.0-alpha.53

## 中文

- 放映站把封存柜的入口移回首页顶部左上角「映汐」二字：连续长按两秒，再由设备核验身份；札记里的站名回到纸上。
- 片窗打开前优先读取原片的宽高与旋转信息，按画面方向入场；后台播放器接过新片时也沿用这份判断。
- 影集里的电影目录更顺手：以片名归档、且只有一部正片时，点目录即可放映；剧集与多版本目录仍展开片单。
- 信源页的分册与添加按钮和标题对齐；整理分册换上透光玻璃，昼夜各有底色，大字与键盘展开时也能滚动整理。
- 放映失败时可复制检修记录，带上编码标记与设备解码能力，方便核对原片；记录不带信源地址、片名与账户凭据。

> 册名写在纸上，片罐还放在原来的格子里。换一行索引，不必搬动整架影像。——阿汐

## English

- The cabinet entrance returns to “映汐” at the upper left of Home: hold for two seconds, then let the device verify entry. The station name in the diary returns to the page.
- Before opening the window, the projection station first checks the original video's dimensions and rotation to choose its direction. A background player receiving another film uses the same hint.
- Movie folders in the gallery open more directly: a folder named for a film with one feature starts that film on a tap. Episodes and multiple editions keep their file lists.
- Volume and add buttons align with the source heading. Volume management gains translucent glass in both appearances, with scrolling for large text and an open keyboard.
- A failed screening offers a copyable repair record with codec identifiers and device decoder capabilities. Source addresses, film names and account credentials stay out of the record.

> The volume name stays on paper; the film tins stay in their old slots. Changing an index line need not move a shelf of images. — Axi
