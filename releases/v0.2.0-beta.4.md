# 映汐放映站 0.2.0-beta.4 / Yingxi 0.2.0-beta.4

## 中文

【拾荒者信笺】

迟灯没急着收回检修线。他看了看仍在转动的刻度，把记录纸向灯下挪了挪。

【修缮记录】

1. 调整兼容解码的首帧等待：流式读取仍在推进时继续等待，避免把慢读误判成解码失败；读取停滞超时后收回连接，停止空等。
2. 阿里云盘原片遇到“请求无效”回应时，重新取址一次，沿原来的字节位置继续读取。
3. 频段抄本添上一次改记：划掉的刻度仍能辨认，空格留给尚未记下的读数。

「先看刻度还走不走。」——迟灯

## English

[Scavenger letter]

Chideng leaves the repair lead in place. He checks the dial that is still moving and draws the record sheet closer to the lamp.

[Repairs]

1. Refine the wait for compatibility playback's first frame. Streaming reads that keep making progress can continue, so a slow read is not mistaken for a decoding failure. A stalled opening times out and releases its connection.
2. If Aliyun rejects an original-file request as invalid, fetch a fresh address once and continue from the same byte position.
3. Add a correction to the Frequency Logbook: crossed-out marks stay legible, and blanks remain for readings not yet recorded.

“See whether the dial is still moving first.” — Chideng
