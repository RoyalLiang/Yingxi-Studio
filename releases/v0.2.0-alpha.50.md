# 映汐放映站 0.2.0-alpha.50 / Yingxi 0.2.0-alpha.50

## 中文

拾荒者们：

弥途把木板擦净，摊开两张压平的纸签。「路标对读」已放上探索桌，六组记录各留一处要辨认的边界：哪些已有依据，哪些仍待核实。点选后能看到解释，也能先读依据，再作判断。

每张纸签都连着随包原文，作者采用的设定、站内传闻与尚未采用的提案分别写明。地图上的地点也能带你开始相应的一组。离开后保留当前纸签、选择和展开的依据，重摆时会先留一句提醒。

旧片匣、调频与对读共用一张探索桌。练习桌上的纸签摆齐了，没走过的道路仍留虚线。

——弥途把铅笔夹回木板边：「纸上对一遍，鞋底还得再走一遍。」

## English

Scavengers,

Mitu wipes the board and lays out two flattened slips. Read the waymarks is now on the exploration desk: six pairs ask what the records establish and what still needs checking. Choose to read the explanation, or read the evidence first and decide later.

Each slip opens its bundled original, keeping adopted canon, station hearsay and unadopted proposals distinct. A place on the map can start its corresponding pair. Leaving keeps the current slips, choices and open explanations; laying them out again asks first.

Case sorting, signal tuning and comparison share one exploration desk. Neatly matched slips leave unwalked roads dotted.

— Mitu clips the pencil to the board: “Matching paper still leaves the journey to my boots.”
