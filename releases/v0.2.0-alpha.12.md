# 映汐播放器 0.2.0-alpha.12 / Yingxi 0.2.0-alpha.12

## 中文

- 拆掉首页的重复路牌，换上浅色片架，把正在看的片子搬到手边。
- 长名字、弹窗和按钮重新排齐，窄屏与大字各留位置；来源、文件和分组选中后留下清楚的记号。
- 快进预览复用独立取帧资源、预热并缓存画面，少跑回头路；取消旧请求后仍可继续取帧。
- 源码和安装包按同一版本封箱，补上可校验的源码 ZIP。
- 本版说明只记改动，应用内的记录也随版本一同更新。

## English

- Clear the duplicate signposts from Home. Put a light film shelf in their place and keep unfinished films close at hand.
- Straighten long names, dialogs and buttons, leaving room for narrow screens and larger text. Mark selected sources, files and groups clearly.
- Reuse an independent preview reader, warm it up and cache its frames to cut repeat trips. Keep frame extraction recoverable after cancellation.
- Pack the source and installer from the same version, with a verifiable source ZIP alongside the APK.
- Keep release notes to changes only, and carry the same record into the app with each version.
