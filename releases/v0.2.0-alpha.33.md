# 映汐放映室 0.2.0-alpha.33 / Yingxi 0.2.0-alpha.33

## 中文

【拾荒者信笺】

这次修缮了停在开场前的放映设备，也整理了片架和札记。封存的影像有了自己的入口，路标以你选定的目录为界。

【修缮记录】

1. HEVC 解码器一直等待首帧时，会尝试软件解码接续；缓冲中的胶片标记开始转动。
2. 来源根目录成为浏览边界，面包屑、返回与旧快捷入口不再带你越到外层。
3. 新增封存柜，可封存目录或单集，从公共片架、历史与今日拾获收起。验证身份后开柜，退到后台重新落锁并停止封存影像播放；封存项不自动查询在线海报和字幕。
4. 档案室入口收进札记，住民头像移到右上角；影视卡片的选中描边只围住海报。
5. 竖屏播放的倍速、音字幕、选集与转向等操作移到右侧栏，相关弹窗收窄并支持滚动。
6. 幸存者据点与外部信号塔分开登记，各自选择连接类型；设置页补记安装包与缓存占用。
7. 档案补入一条片罐侧签短笺，放映站词条同步记下两人的日常分工。

> 片罐的标签写在侧面。盖子磨花了，名字也不会跟着丢。——阿汐

## English

[Scavenger’s letter]

This letter brings repairs for equipment that stalled before the opening frame, along with tidier shelves and a revised diary. Sealed images have their own entrance, and the signposts stop at the directory you choose.

[Repairs this time]

1. HEVC playback tries software decoding when the native decoder keeps waiting for its first frame. The buffering reel now turns.
2. The selected source root bounds browsing, breadcrumbs, back navigation, and older shortcuts.
3. The Sealed Cabinet holds directories or individual episodes apart from public shelves, history, and today's finds. Device authentication opens it; backgrounding locks it again and stops sealed playback. Sealed items skip automatic online poster and subtitle lookup.
4. The archive entrance moves into the diary, with the resident's portrait at the upper right. Selection outlines now frame poster artwork alone.
5. Portrait playback actions move to a right sidebar. Speed, track, and episode panels use narrower, scrollable layouts.
6. Survivor Outposts and External Signal Towers have separate registration entries and connection choices. Settings now report installed package and cache sizes.
7. The archives gain a note about labeling film cans, and the station dictionary records the pair's daily work.

> Put the label on the side of the film can. A worn lid should not take its name with it. — Axi
