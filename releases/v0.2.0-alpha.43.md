# 映汐放映站 0.2.0-alpha.43 / Yingxi 0.2.0-alpha.43

## 中文

[拾荒者信笺]

迟灯在桌边并排摆好几只浅铁盘，夹上片号。几处可以一起整理，缺的那一格，仍要认真找回来。

[本次修缮]

1. 阿里云盘原片可以分路读取，先到的片段先送进放映机；换了位置，旧路上的预读及时收住。
2. 已读片段可以留在站内缓存，接近片尾时也能在 Wi-Fi 下先接下一集的开头；设置中可选读取路数、开关缓存与清理片段。
3. 临近到期的线路提早接洽，断开的片段从缺口续上；检修纸分别记下实际传输与缓存读取。

> 几盘可以一起理，片号得一格格对。缺的地方先留白，不能算齐了。 —— 迟灯

## English

[Scavenger’s letter]

Chideng lines up a few shallow iron trays and clips on their case numbers. Several trays can be sorted together; a missing frame still deserves a careful search.

[Station repairs]

1. Aliyun original files can arrive over parallel routes, with incoming bytes passed straight to the projector. Seeking cancels pre-reading along the old route.
2. Read segments may stay in the station cache, and Wi-Fi can bring in the next episode’s opening near the end. Settings offer connection counts, cache controls and segment cleanup.
3. Expiring links are renewed early and interrupted segments resume at the gap. The repair sheet records actual transfers and cache reads separately.

> Sort the trays together, but check the frame numbers in order. Leave a gap where a frame is missing; do not call it complete. — Chideng
