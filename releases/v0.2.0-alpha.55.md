# 映汐放映站 0.2.0-alpha.55 / Yingxi 0.2.0-alpha.55

## 中文

- 放映站收拢 SDR 兼容光路上的重复检修与细碎读取，铺满时让片窗贴合屏幕；播放途中也能打开检修记录，查看掉帧、音画差与读取状况。
- 同目录的字幕与配音音轨会一同列入片架，分别载入、共用播放进度；本机音轨也可单独加入。常见音轨多了一条软件解码后路，损坏的外挂不会把主片留在报错处。
- 找不到射手字幕时，可以沿标明 Bing 的路牌按片名上网搜寻；配音也有独立的 Bing 搜索入口，取得文件后可带回播放器载入。
- 片窗更早读取影片朝向，拖动预览提前整理索引；滑动进度时中央控件收起，原先隐藏的控件不会在松手后突然亮起。
- 字幕留在屏幕可见边沿内，长句跨越跳转仍能显示；封存柜的影集封面改用贴合圆角的小幅按压反馈。

> 纸上的小记号，只替我们少翻几遍架子。——迟灯

## English

- The projection station trims repeated checks and small reads along the SDR compatibility path, fitting the filled window to the screen. Repair records can now be opened during playback to inspect dropped frames, A/V drift and source reads.
- Matching subtitles and dubbed audio beside a film load separately on the same playback clock; local audio can also be added. Common audio gains a software decoding fallback, and damaged sidecars no longer leave the main film stopped at an error.
- When Shooter has no match, a sign marked Bing opens a title-based subtitle search. Dubbed audio has its own Bing search entry; bring a matching file back to load it.
- The window reads film direction sooner and prepares preview metadata ahead of scrubbing. Centre controls stay tucked away during a seek, and hidden controls stay hidden after release.
- Subtitles remain within the visible screen edges, including long cues across seeks. Sealed film covers now respond with a small press that follows their rounded outline.

> Small marks on paper save a few trips along the shelves. — Chideng
