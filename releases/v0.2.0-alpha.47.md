# 映汐放映站 0.2.0-alpha.47 / Yingxi 0.2.0-alpha.47

## 中文

[拾荒者信笺]

阿汐把旧事夹回档案架，片匣的封面重新留给画面。

1. 海报左上角只留下白色格式文字，收起信源、大小与底色，让片架更清爽。
2. 札记下方的档案室入口缩减上下留白，随文字高度舒展。
3. 据点大事记归入档案室；探索继续保留来信、地点与词条。

> 我把散开的旧事夹回档案架，只在纸脊上添一个小小的片号。封面留给画面，想找来处的人，翻开里面那一页就好。 —— 阿汐

## English

[Scavenger’s letter]

Axi returns the loose records to the archive shelf and leaves the case covers to their pictures.

1. Posters keep only a white format label in the upper left. Source names, sizes, and label backgrounds leave the cover clear.
2. The archive entrance below the journal trims its vertical space and follows the text height.
3. Station chronicles now live in the archives. Exploration keeps its letters, places, and terms.

> I return the loose station records to the archive shelf and add a small case number to each paper spine. The cover belongs to the picture; anyone looking for its origin can turn to the page inside. — Axi
