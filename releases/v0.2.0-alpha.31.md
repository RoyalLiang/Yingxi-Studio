# 映汐放映室 0.2.0-alpha.31 / Yingxi 0.2.0-alpha.31

## 中文

【拾荒者信笺】

长潮退去，能用的东西都得留着修。放映站这回先检修放映机，再把门口混在一起的两块路牌分开。

【放映站修缮记录】

1. 放映机重新排好工序：先用设备解码，HEVC 确实解码失败或迟迟没有画面时才交给备用解码。画面输出切换出错时，从原进度接回放映。播放时不再自动提取预览和封面；拖动进度条时仍可查看预览。
2. 探索留下地点图谱和据点足迹，档案室收好住民档案与来信，各有各的门牌。
3. 更新柜台重新打牢：检查、下载和安装操作固定在底部，铺上独立底板，阅读长信时也留在原处。
4. 避难所里的影片与剧集目录换上海报墙，可读取目录海报和在线匹配的封面。数字集名沿上层目录查找剧名，进入目录后显示它自己的名字。
5. 信笺改用放映站的修缮记录写法，收起不合时宜的套话，把修了什么、入口在哪儿写清楚。

> 旧零件别急着扔。先认清哪块该干什么活。——迟灯

## English

[Scavenger's letter]

After the Long Tide, anything still useful is worth repairing. This round starts with the projector, then separates the two signs that had been pointing through the same door.

[Station repair log]

1. Put decoding back in order: try the device decoder first, and bring in the HEVC backup after a decoding failure or a persistent missing picture. Recover an output switch from the current playback position. Playback no longer starts an extra decoder to prepare previews or covers. Seeking still offers frame previews.
2. Keep the place map and station footprints in Explore. Keep people and their letters in the archives, each behind its own sign.
3. Secure the update counter: checks, downloads and installation stay in a fixed bottom panel with its own backing, even while a long letter scrolls.
4. Give shelter videos and series folders a poster wall, using folder artwork and matched online covers. Numbered episodes take their series name from the parent folders; opened directories display their own names.
5. Write these letters as station repair logs, with plain accounts of the work and where to find it.

> Don't throw the old parts away yet. First work out which one belongs on which job. — Chideng
