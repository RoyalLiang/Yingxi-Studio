# 映汐播放器 0.2.0-alpha.6 / Yingxi 0.2.0-alpha.6

## 中文

### 更新内容

- 应用名称更新为“映汐播放器”，同步首页、设置与播放通知。
- WebDAV 添加默认展示阿里云盘、百度网盘官方图标；支持打开已安装 App、未安装提示及官网入口，停用或无法启动时单独提示不可用。
- 普通用户界面不要求填写开发者 AppID/AppKey。开放平台申请、公开客户端标识与回调地址由开发者配置，提供项目模板、校验脚本和申请说明。
- 保留手动 WebDAV 地址、认证、浏览与播放；编辑来源直接进入地址表单。

### 验证与限制

- Android 15/16 模拟器验证网盘入口、未安装提示、手动 WebDAV、返回导航、播放界面与设置；构建和 Lint 通过。真实官方网盘 App 的登录授权与真实 NAS 未验证。
- 官方 OAuth、授权回跳、令牌管理及原生网盘文件读取尚未实现；打开 App 不代表已授权或已添加来源。填好开发者模板也不会自动启用未完成的授权链路。
- 沿用正式发布签名，可从 alpha.5 覆盖升级。iOS 首包以独立的 ios-v0.1.0-alpha.1 版本及验证报告交付。

## English

### Changes

- Renamed the visible app to 映汐播放器 (Yingxi Player), including home, settings and playback notifications.
- WebDAV setup starts with official Aliyunpan and Baidu Netdisk icons, installed-app launch, missing-app prompts and official website links. Disabled or unlaunchable apps have a separate unavailable state.
- Developer registration belongs in project configuration, with a public-client template, validator and application guide. End users are not asked to enter developer IDs or keys.
- Manual WebDAV setup, authentication, browsing and playback remain available; editing a source opens its address form directly.

### Validation and limitations

- Android 15/16 emulator checks cover provider entries, missing apps, manual WebDAV, back navigation, player visuals and settings. Build and Lint pass. Real provider authorization and physical NAS behavior remain unverified.
- Official OAuth, callbacks, tokens and native cloud-drive access are pending. Launching a provider app does not authorize or add a media source, and completing the configuration template does not enable the unfinished authorization flow.
- The established release signing identity is retained for upgrades from alpha.5. The initial iOS package is delivered separately as ios-v0.1.0-alpha.1 with its own validation report.
