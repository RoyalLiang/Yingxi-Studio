# 映汐放映站 0.2.0-alpha.60 / Yingxi 0.2.0-alpha.60

## 中文

- 放映站的检修回执留在片窗里：预览播放快照、了解公开范围并勾选同意后，点击即可上报，送达时显示仓库回执编号。
- 没有取得收条的检修纸会留下标记。再打开时可先查上次回执，再决定是否寄出新的一份。
我在检修纸右下角留了一块空白，迟灯把铜尺横在桌边；有了收条再记号码。寄出一张纸，和修好一部旧片，是两回事。——阿汐

## English

- The projection station keeps repair notes inside the player. Preview the playback snapshot, read its public scope and give consent, then submit it and receive the repository receipt number on delivery.
- A note without a receipt keeps its marker. Reopen it to check the previous delivery before choosing to send a new one.
I leave a blank corner on the repair note. Chideng lays his copper ruler along the desk: write the number when a receipt arrives. Sending a note and mending an old film are two different things. — Axi
