# 映汐放映站 0.2.0-alpha.41 / Yingxi 0.2.0-alpha.41

## 中文

【拾荒者信笺】

片架旁的旧路牌重新写好了。来处写得明白，找片匣的人便能少绕一点路。

【本次修缮】

1. 菜单里的「避难所」改称「信源」。远处接来的影像，仍从这里登记、翻找。
2. 登记信源时，「外部信号塔」改称「信号塔」，与「幸存者据点」各守一页；相关路牌一并换好。
3. 探索与档案室收起最下方的附记，留下读信、翻档案的空处。
4. 初次进站时，两路线条汇入站徽，再揭开首页；轻触即可略过，回到站内不必重走一遍。
5. 检修记录补上送进放映机与放到幕上的画幅数，也记下解码中断，便于寻找久候无画面的断处。
6. 本版信笺沿用放映站的称谓与修缮口吻，把变动写成站内人听得懂的话。

路牌写清来处，细账夹进簿里。找片匣的人，不必先读完一页告示。——阿汐

## English

[Scavenger's letter]

The old sign beside the film rack has been rewritten. With the way clearly marked, anyone looking for a film case has one less turn to take.

[Repairs this time]

1. The menu's Shelter entry is now Sources. Images brought in from afar are still registered and browsed here.
2. External signal tower is now Signal tower. It keeps its own page beside Survivor outpost when registering a source, with the surrounding signs updated too.
3. The closing notes at the foot of Explore and the Archive have been put away, leaving room for letters and records.
4. On the first visit after launch, two lines meet at the station mark before the home page is revealed. Tap to skip; returning to the station does not replay the arrival.
5. Repair records now count frames fed into the projector and shown on the screen, alongside decoder interruptions, to help trace a long wait without a picture.
6. This letter uses the station's names and repair-room voice to tell what has changed in words its residents would use.

Put the way in plain sight and keep the fine print in the ledger. No one should need a page of notices to find a film case. — Axi
