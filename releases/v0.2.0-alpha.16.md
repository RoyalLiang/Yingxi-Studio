# 映汐播放器 0.2.0-alpha.16 / Yingxi 0.2.0-alpha.16

## 中文

【拾荒者信笺 · 映汐放映站 0.2.0-alpha.16】

这回重新认了片架上几张面孔的名字，也把放映时挡在眼前的小东西挪到了两旁。

【修缮记录】

1. 修复“小丑2”一类别名的海报匹配：沿正式片名查找，保留年份和续集编号，剧集不再认成同名电影。
2. 同一目录里的不同电影不再共用一张通用封面；旧匹配留下的海报会重新查找，过期结果也会更新。
3. 全屏播放的倍速、音字幕、画面比例、选集和显示方向靠右排列，时间留在左侧，窄屏与大字号可以换行。
4. 音字幕和选集面板展开后，轻点一旁的影片画面即可收起；面板里的空白不会误关，列表仍可滚动选择。
5. 亮度与音量改用左右两侧的半透明竖向刻度，让画面中央空出来。

【放映站手记】

刻度条搬到两旁后，放映员终于不用隔着一张亮度牌认字幕了。

## English

[Scavenger's letter · Yingxi Projection Station 0.2.0-alpha.16]

A few faces on the shelf have their names back. A few small things have also moved to the sides of the picture.

[Repairs]

1. Fix poster matching for aliases such as “小丑2” (Joker 2). Follow the official title while preserving release years and sequel numbers; series no longer borrow a namesake film's poster.
2. Different films in a shared folder no longer inherit the same generic cover. Posters from older matching rules are checked again, and expired results are refreshed.
3. Playback speed, audio and subtitles, picture sizing, episodes, and orientation sit on the right of the fullscreen toolbar, with time on the left. Narrow screens and larger text can wrap the actions.
4. Tap the picture beside the audio/subtitle or episode panel to close it. Blank space inside a panel keeps it open, while lists remain scrollable and selectable.
5. Brightness and volume now appear as translucent vertical gauges on the left and right, leaving the center of the picture clear.

[Station journal]

With the gauges moved aside, the projectionist can finally read the subtitles without peering around a brightness sign.
