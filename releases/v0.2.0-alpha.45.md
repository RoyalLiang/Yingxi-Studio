# 映汐放映站 0.2.0-alpha.45 / Yingxi 0.2.0-alpha.45

## 中文

[拾荒者信笺]

片匣角上的来处抄清了，放映机也重新接住快转的节拍。

1. 修好倍速指令绕过放映机的缺口，1.5 倍与更快放映时，画面的节拍跟着一起提速。
2. 信源、视频格式和影片大小收进海报；片架只留片名，不再重复写「文件夹」或大小。目录收起非视频文件，左上角只保留信源名称。
3. 封存柜的路标搬到拾荒记录旁，换上箱柜入口；封存片架、封存影像和开柜提示沿用站内称谓，原片仍留在原处。
4. 分册登记换成统一的信纸，先选分册再修改，同一处不再挤着两套选择样式。

> 我把来处写在片匣角上。写下一个地名，不等于那条路已经走通；地图上的空白，仍得留着。 —— 弥途

[Scavenger’s letter]

The case corners carry clearer origins, and the projector follows the faster beat again.

1. Repair the missing speed handoff to the video renderer, so pictures follow 1.5× and faster playback.
2. Source, file format and video size move onto posters. Titles keep their own space, without repeated folder or size labels. Directories hide non-video files, while the header keeps the source name.
3. The sealed cabinet moves beside the scavenging records with a cabinet-shaped entry. Sealing and access prompts use the station’s vocabulary; original files stay in place.
4. Collections share one consistent register: choose a collection, then edit it, without competing selection controls.

> I wrote the place of origin on the case’s corner. Naming a place does not mean the road is clear; the blank on the map must stay. — Mitu
