# 映汐放映站 0.2.0-beta.5 / Yingxi 0.2.0-beta.5

## 中文

【拾荒者信笺】

阿汐把木夹停在抄本当页。迟灯翻过旧读数，终于找到让放映机反复停步的那道手续。

【修缮记录】

1. 减少流式播放中重复计算信源身份、反复查阅片架登记的开销，缩短高码率影片的解析与首帧等待；信源变更、移除与授权撤回仍及时生效。
2. 自动横竖屏只记住已读到的影片尺寸，清理旧方向提示的影响，让画面就位后按影片比例展开。
3. 频段抄本添了交班时的页夹记录：旧读数留在原页，临时木夹用后归位。

> “页码念清，就不用每翻一行都从封面找起。”——阿汐

## English

[Scavenger letter]

Axi rests a wooden clip on the open page. Chideng works through the old readings and finds the repeated check that has kept the projector waiting.

[Repairs]

1. Reduce repeated source-identity calculations and shelf-record lookups during streaming, shortening parsing and first-frame waits for high-bitrate films. Source changes, removal and permission withdrawal still take effect promptly.
2. Remember automatic orientation only after the film’s dimensions arrive, and retire older provisional hints so the picture can open to its own proportions.
3. Add a shift-change note to the Frequency Logbook: old readings keep their place, and the temporary page clip returns to the shelves.

> “Read the page number clearly. Then every line needn’t begin at the cover.” — Axi
