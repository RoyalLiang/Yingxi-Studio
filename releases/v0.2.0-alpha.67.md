## 中文

【拾荒者信笺】

换一卷胶片，等下一束光。

【修缮记录】

1. 字幕驿站标题与选项分行布局，避免窄屏挤压；自定义搜寻地址使用统一圆角输入框，保存入口收进框内，保留地址校验。
2. 画质入口改为靠近顶部按钮的小型菜单，选择后自动收起；保留原画、平台档位和片内视频轨选择。
3. 播放与检修等待恢复旋转圆环；检修完成继续使用无底板勾选动画。
4. 选集、上一集、下一集及切集手势立即切换标题、清除旧进度并遮住上一集画面，新片首帧到达后显示；兼容音频与读取失败的结束状态。
5. 缓存命中直接读取并校验一个载荷缓冲区，非对齐回放直接从已验证缓存复制到读取窗口，减少重复整段分配；保留哈希、过期、容量及账号隔离校验。
6. 采用选定的奶油莓粉方案，统一设置分组、播放面板及选中态；更新下载只保留按钮内进度与百分比，底部不显示容量大小。

## English

[Scavenger letter]

Change the reel, then wait for its first light.

[Repairs]

1. Subtitle station titles sit above their choices on narrow screens. Custom search addresses use a rounded field with an inline Save action and the existing URL validation.
2. The quality button opens a compact anchored menu that closes after selection, retaining original, provider and embedded video-track choices.
3. Playback and report waiting use a rotating ring again. Report completion keeps its transparent check animation.
4. Episode selection, Previous, Next and episode gestures immediately update the title, clear stale progress and cover the previous image until the next actual frame. Audio and source-error states also end the wait.
5. Verified cache hits use one payload buffer. Unaligned replay copies directly from the verified segment into its read window, removing full-segment intermediates while retaining integrity, expiry, quota and account separation.
6. Apply the selected cream-and-berry design to settings, playback panels and selection states. Update downloads show progress and percentage inside one button, without a capacity label below it.
