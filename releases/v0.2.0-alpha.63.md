## 中文

【拾荒者信笺】

- 兼容播放的画质列表只提供当前后端已启用的视频解码轨道；未放行的软件格式不会被列为可播放档位。

[Repairs]
- Compatibility playback offers quality tracks only when their video decoder is enabled in the current backend. Disabled software formats are excluded from playable choices.
