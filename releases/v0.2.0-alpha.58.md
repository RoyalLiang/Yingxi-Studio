# 映汐放映站 0.2.0-alpha.58 / Yingxi 0.2.0-alpha.58

## 中文

- 放映站的音轨与字幕先写语种，再留编号；普通话、粤语和简繁标记各归其位，没留下语种的旧片也会照实留白。
- 音轨与字幕各有一枚片窗按钮，直接挑选，不再挤在播放选项里；竖屏把画质与画幅收进更多操作。
- 缺字幕时先查片旁文件，再按所选来源自动匹配资源；默认 SubHD，可在站务切换。可载入的字幕接回片窗，匹配页留给尚需在网站完成的下载。
- 进度预览记住看过的位置，转动屏幕后仍可复用；拖向远处时等待对应画面，封存时一并收起缓存。

> 看不清的字，不必急着补成一句话。——阿汐

## English

- At the projection station, audio and subtitle choices lead with their language, followed by a track number. Mandarin, Cantonese and script labels keep their distinctions; missing language tags remain clear.
- Audio and subtitles each have a direct player button. In portrait, quality and picture fit move into More.
- Missing subtitles are matched automatically after checking nearby files. SubHD is the default source and can be changed in station settings. Available files return to the film; matched pages remain available when a download needs the website.
- Seek previews retain visited positions across rotation, wait for the right image when moving farther away, and clear their shared cache when the sealed cabinet locks.

> An unreadable word need not become a sentence in a hurry. — Axi
