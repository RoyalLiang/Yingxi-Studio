# 映汐放映站 0.2.0-alpha.57 / Yingxi 0.2.0-alpha.57

## 中文

- 放映站替旧片补齐接线：H.264、VP8/VP9 与 MPEG 视频在系统解码受阻后接续软件解码，几类旧封装交给原有兼容放映机。
- P5 的 SDR 兼容光路添了一段短帧周转，让解码与映色接续工作；正片读取接回原有预读与配置，慢附件另排一路。
- 检修纸补回了音频解码器的名字，便于辨认片窗里的声音来自哪一段接线。

> 响声从哪儿来，要一处一处听。——迟灯

## English

- The projection station reconnects more old films: H.264, VP8/VP9 and MPEG video can continue through software decoding after a system failure, while several older containers share the existing compatibility projector.
- P5 SDR playback gains a short frame handoff between decoding and color mapping. Original streams retain their read-ahead settings, with slow attachment pickups on a separate queue.
- Repair notes now keep the audio decoder's name, helping trace which connection carries the sound.

> We listen to one place at a time to learn where a rattle begins. — Chideng
