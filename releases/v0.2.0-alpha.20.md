# 映汐播放器 0.2.0-alpha.20 / Yingxi 0.2.0-alpha.20

## 中文

【拾荒者信笺 · 映汐放映站 0.2.0-alpha.20】

阿汐把一张折了很久的地图铺在柜台上。迟灯认出了盐环的站名，弥途却先拿起铅笔，把没走过的几条路改成了虚线。

> 没走过的路，我会画成虚线。——弥途

【本次实装】
1. 版本页新增「放映站档案」。影像拾荒者阿汐、守夜人迟灯、路标师弥途留下了自己的名字、肖像与语录；轻触信纸，可以听他们换一句话。
2. 第一张世界图谱展开：点击地点可阅读档案，实线记录往返信路，虚线保留远方传闻。版本足迹会记下信笺走过的路，人物与关系也有了共同的档案。

【修缮记录】
3. 沿用每次下载各自独立的安装包，并补上摘要标记；本版将校验后的内容交给 Android 独立安装会话，重新核对当前安装版本，取消或失败后即时回到版本页反馈。
4. 检查更新固定在版本页上方，当前信笺直接展开，只有正文区域滚动。段落、修缮条目与人物引语分开排版，发布页入口收进右上角更多菜单。
5. 发版信笺沿用同一份中英正文，人物说话的习惯与图谱一起维护。适合时让他们接上一句，修好的东西仍要说清。

【放映站手记】
迟灯把冷茶挪开，给地图空出一点地方。幕布之外的世界很大，这封信先从灯还亮着的地方寄出。

> A road I haven’t walked gets a dotted line. — Mitu

[New arrivals]
1. Add Station archives to the version page. Image scavenger Axi, night keeper Chideng and wayfinder Mitu now have names, native portraits and their own lines. Tap their paper to hear another thought.
2. Open the first world atlas. Tap places to read their records: solid lines show letter routes, dotted lines preserve distant rumors. Version footprints and a shared record keep the cast and their connections together.

[Repairs]
3. Keep a separate payload for every download and add its digest to the identity. Pass verified bytes to an independent Android install session, recheck the installed version, and return cancellation or failure feedback immediately to the version page.
4. Keep update controls at the top of the version page and show the current letter directly below. Only its body scrolls. Give paragraphs, repairs and character quotations their own typography; move the release-page link into the top overflow menu.
5. Share the same bilingual letter between the app and releases. Maintain character voices with the atlas, bringing in a short line when it fits while keeping the actual repairs clear.
