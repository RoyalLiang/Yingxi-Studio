# 映汐放映室 0.2.0-alpha.36 / Yingxi 0.2.0-alpha.36

## 中文

[拾荒者信笺]

登记台收回了两块占地方的牌子。下回来时，点右上角的加号，就能翻开同一本登记簿。

1. 避难所恢复统一添加入口。幸存者据点与外部信号塔放回添加弹层的分类页签，协议由下拉选择；两边填写的地址与账号各自保留。
2. WebDAV 读取不再先探一字节再重新接线，起播与续读直接沿所需位置取得影像，减少往返等待。
3. 阿汐给登记簿系上了厚纸夹页，档案室添了一句值班叮嘱。

> 登记簿的夹页别抽走。两边的来信各有去处，回头才找得到。——阿汐

## English

[Scavenger's letter]

The two oversized signs are off the registration desk. Next time, tap the plus at the top right to open the same logbook.

1. Shelters share one add entry again. Survivor outposts and external signal towers sit inside the add sheet as category tabs, with a protocol dropdown and separate address and account drafts.
2. WebDAV no longer probes one byte before reconnecting. Startup and resumed reads request the needed position directly, cutting network round trips.
3. Axi ties card dividers into the logbook and leaves a new reminder in the archives.

> Leave the dividers in the logbook. Each bundle of letters has its place; we need to find them again. — Axi
