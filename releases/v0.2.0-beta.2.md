# 映汐放映站 0.2.0-beta.2 / Yingxi 0.2.0-beta.2

## 中文

【拾荒者信笺】

旧登记册里的散页，继续归回原来的片架。

【修缮记录】

1. 承接 Alpha 旧稿中的铅封路签、片架索引绳与频段抄本，补入六则值班札记和三条语录。沿用已发布札记与语录的编号、正文和作者，旧稿里重号的纸条另编新号。
2. 统一放映速度的使用与保存范围：0.25 到 4 倍速在重开放映时继续保留，行囊备份使用相同范围。
3. 行囊备份完整收存自动读取和现有手动读取档位；恢复时拒绝超出设置范围的数值，拒绝前保留现有资料。
4. 对齐地下据点、深层避难所及第二章的资料说明，修正仍指向已收起词典入口的文字；探索沿用章节来信、地点图谱与三项离线练习，生态图鉴保留创作稿标识。
5. 据点足迹按版本顺序归位，保留旧版信笺；沿用现有影片身份、观看进度、放映单、短记和信源权限边界。

> 「同一张纸，别因为换了册子就改掉原来的号。」——阿汐

The loose pages from the old register return to their shelf.

[Repairs]

1. Carry over the lead-sealed route tally, shelf index cord and frequency logbook from the Alpha drafts, along with six journal notes and three quotes. Released notes and quotes retain their IDs, words and authors; conflicting draft numbers receive new IDs.
2. Align playback and saved speed settings from 0.25 to 4 times normal speed. Reopening playback retains the selected speed, and Satchel Backup uses the same range.
3. Satchel Backup retains automatic reading and every supported manual reading mode. Restore rejects unsupported values before changing existing records.
4. Align the descriptions of the underground outpost, deep shelters and Chapter II. Correct references to the retired dictionary entry; Explore keeps its letters, map and three offline exercises, while the ecology gallery remains labelled as a creative draft.
5. Put station chronicles in version order and retain old release letters. Existing film identities, progress, screening lists, short notes and source permission boundaries continue to apply.

> “Moving a page to another register is no reason to change its number.” — Axi
