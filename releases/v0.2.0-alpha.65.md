# 映汐放映站 0.2.0-alpha.65 / Yingxi 0.2.0-alpha.65

## 中文

【拾荒者信笺】

片架短脚下的旧纸换成了一片薄铜垫。阿汐把取出的纸抚平，迟灯给片匣重新留出位置，正在放映的旧片仍沿着原来的边沿走。

【修缮记录】

1. 兼容播放的拖动、调速、选轨和退出改为依次处理，片窗交接时保留正在使用的画面资源，让控制及时回应。
2. 同目录字幕和音轨随开片一并准备；播放中补入或更换字幕时保留主片缓冲，手动选择的内嵌字幕不再被默认外挂字幕顶回去。
3. WebDAV 遇到变化的文件版本标记时提示重新打开，避免把前后两个版本的片段拼在一起。
4. 回收分段缓存后，回收前的下载不再把旧片段写回来；新的读取仍按设置积累缓存。
5. 取消缩略图时撤销等待并关闭读取；缓慢打开的文档不再占住后续取帧的通道。
6. 已保存海报在片架刷新时复用，替换、删除或清空后同步失效；从放映记录补回片架时一次合并，并保留原发现时间。
7. 更换外挂音轨沿用当前进度、倍速与暂停状态；损坏的可选音轨不会抢先把主片转去不适用的放映后端。

“垫稳以后，再轻推一次。灯已经亮着，也得让人能把手收回来。”——迟灯

## English

[Scavenger letter]

A thin copper shim replaces the old paper beneath the shelf's short leg. Axi smooths the recovered sheets while Chideng leaves room for the next reel. The film already playing keeps its course.

[Repairs]

1. Compatibility playback handles seeking, speed, track selection, and exit in order. The window retains its picture resources until the handoff completes, keeping controls responsive.
2. Same-folder subtitles and audio are prepared with the film. Late subtitle changes preserve the main film's buffer, and an explicit embedded-subtitle choice takes priority over an attached default.
3. WebDAV asks to reopen a film when its file-version markers change, preventing segments from different versions from being mixed.
4. Downloads started before a cache clear cannot restore the old segments. New reads can still populate the cache according to settings.
5. Cancelling a thumbnail withdraws pending work and closes its reader. A document that opens slowly no longer occupies the frame-decoding lane.
6. Saved posters are reused across shelf refreshes and invalidated on replacement or removal. Restored history is merged into the shelf once, preserving first-seen dates.
7. External audio changes preserve position, speed, and pause state. A damaged optional audio track no longer sends the main film to an unsuitable playback backend.
