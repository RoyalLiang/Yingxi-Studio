# 映汐放映站 0.2.0-alpha.46 / Yingxi 0.2.0-alpha.46

## 中文

[拾荒者信笺]
灯罩压低一格，走过的片架也有了自己的脚印。

1. 守夜人模式添上跟随系统、日间与守夜三档，片库、信源、札记与设置一起换上合适的光线。
2. 拾荒记录可切换为拾荒者路线图，沿本机观看时间重访影片，续播、整理与封存仍在手边。
3. 播放选项接入阿里云盘返回的完整清晰度；原画仍是默认，平台提供多个完整档位或真实自适应列表时可选自动，按实际播放情况降低清晰度。短预览不充作整部影片。
4. 云盘播放链接被拒绝时有次数受限的重新取址，沿用当前队列、位置与倍速；原画片段与转码放映分开读取。

夜里我把灯罩压低一格，检修纸看得清，旁人的眼睛也能歇一会儿。走到哪一格，就记哪一格。
—— 迟灯

## English

[Scavenger’s letter]
The lampshade drops one notch, and visited shelves gain their own footprints.

1. Night Watch adds system, day and night appearances across the library, sources, journal and settings.
2. Scavenging records can become a viewing route through local history, keeping resume, organization and sealing close at hand.
3. Playback options accept full picture variants returned by Aliyun Drive. Original remains the default; Auto appears when the provider supplies multiple full variants or a real adaptive playlist, lowering quality when playback needs it. Short previews never stand in for a full film.
4. Rejected cloud playback links receive bounded renewal while keeping the queue, position and speed. Original segments and transcoded playback use separate reading paths.

At night I lower the lampshade one notch. The repair sheet stays legible and passing eyes can rest. I mark only the steps actually taken.
— Chideng
