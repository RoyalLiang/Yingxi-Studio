# 映汐放映站 0.2.0-alpha.62 / Yingxi 0.2.0-alpha.62

## 中文

【拾荒者信笺】
兼容放映机的片带索引也补齐了，已有的轨道可以逐条选取。

【修缮记录】
- SDR 兼容播放现在读取片源实际的视频轨道、尺寸与码率，支持手动切换及恢复默认选择。
- 画质列表排除封面图与依赖其他轨道的附属画面；上一部影片留下的选择不会遮住本片的默认档。
- 切轨沿用当前播放位置、倍速、音轨和字幕偏好。

## English

[Field letter]
The compatibility projector now has a complete index of the video tracks already on the reel.

[Repairs]
- SDR compatibility playback reads the source’s actual video tracks, dimensions and bitrate, with manual switching and a default selection.
- Quality choices exclude cover art and dependent video tracks. A selection left by the previous film no longer hides the current source’s default.
- Switching keeps the playback position, speed, audio and subtitle preferences.
